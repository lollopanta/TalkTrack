# TalkTrack logo

The TalkTrack mark is the four purple corner brackets around a red recording dot.
Use the mark and wordmark in their supplied proportions; keep at least one bracket
stroke of clear space around them.

## Palette

- Purple: `#6C45F5`
- Recording red: `#E44747`
- App tile lavender: `#F5F0FF`

## Files

- `talktrack-mark.svg`: transparent, scalable symbol master.
- `talktrack-mark-mono.svg` and `talktrack-mark-reversed.svg`: single-color symbol variants.
- `TT_logo.svg`: horizontal wordmark master; `TT_logo_655x200.png` and
  `TT_logo_1300x400.png` are raster previews for places that need PNG.
- `talktrack-app-icon.svg`: square app icon master.
- `talktrack-menubar.svg`: reduced monochrome macOS status-bar mark; its PNG exports are mask icons so macOS adapts them to light and dark menu bars.
- `TT_icon_*.png`: app icon exports used by macOS, the splash screen, and About.
- `talktrack.ico`: multi-size Windows icon used by the taskbar shortcut and tray.
- `favicon.ico`: multi-size favicon export.

Use the full color mark on light backgrounds. Use the purple monochrome mark when
only one ink is available on a light field; use the reversed white mark on dark fields.
